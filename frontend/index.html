Emergency Severity Scale (ESI)

ESI Level 1 (Resuscitation):
Immediate life-saving intervention needed (Code Blue / STEMI / Stroke).
ESI Level 2 (Emergent):
High risk / severe pain / altered vitals. Care within 15 minutes.
ESI Level 3 (Urgent):
Requires 2 or more diagnostic resources (Abdominal CT, IV fluids).
ESI Level 4 & 5 (Routine):
Standard OPD consultation slots & elective daycare procedures.
